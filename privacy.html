Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What Mimo Studio is

Mimo Studio is a private tool that manages a single YouTube channel, "Mimo the Mimic". It is used only by the channel's owner and is not offered to the public.

Data we access through YouTube API Services

  • The channel owner's YouTube account, with the owner's consent via Google OAuth, to upload and update the channel's own videos, thumbnails and playlists.
  • Public comments on the channel's own videos (comment text, display name and like count), to choose ideas for new videos and to reply to them.
  • Channel and video statistics (views, watch time, retention), to plan future videos.

How we use and store it

Data is used only for the purposes above. Comments and statistics are processed automatically and stored only as long as needed to plan upcoming videos, at most 30 days. A commenter's display name may be shown in a video when their suggestion is used, just as it appears publicly on YouTube. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

The OAuth access token for the channel is stored encrypted as a secret in the tool's build system and is used only to act on the channel. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube

Mimo Studio uses YouTube API Services. By interacting with the channel you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Mimo Studio's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your rights

If you want a comment of yours not to be used in a video, or want data about you deleted, write to mimomimicyt@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.
