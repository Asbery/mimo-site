Mimo Studio

Mimo Studio is the production tool behind the YouTube channel Mimo the Mimic, a series of short animated videos about a small treasure-chest creature that turns into whatever viewers ask for in the comments.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to manage this one channel: uploading the channel's own videos, setting their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, and reading comments on the channel's videos to pick the next idea. It is not offered to other users.

Contact: mimomimicyt@gmail.com

▶ Developed with YouTube

How it works: a finished episode is placed in a queue; a scheduled workflow signs in with the channel owner's Google account (OAuth 2.0) and publishes it with videos.insert. The complete upload script is public here: upload-script.txt.
